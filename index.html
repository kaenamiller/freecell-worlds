Play FreeCell free online, in 3D

FreeCell Worlds is classic FreeCell solitaire reimagined on 31 themed 3D tables, with spring-physics cards and a built-in solver. It's free in your browser, with nothing to download.

How to play FreeCell

  1. All 52 cards are dealt face up into eight columns.
  2. Build each of the four foundations by suit, from Ace up to King.
  3. In the columns, stack cards in descending order and alternating colours (for example a red 7 on a black 8).
  4. Use the four free cells to hold one card each while you dig for the cards you need.
  5. Any card can go into an empty column. You win when every card is home.

Controls: drag cards to move them, double-click to send a card home, press H for a hint and U to undo.

Features

  • 31 themed tables: pick a world, or let a random theme start each new game.
  • Voyage mode: seven tables, one journey. No restarts, shared undos, charms to collect, plus a Daily Voyage.
  • Daily Challenge: a new deal every day.
  • Numbered deals: play any deal from #1 to #1,000,000.
  • Built-in solver: powers hints when you're stuck.
  • Stats & achievements: track your best times, moves and streaks.
  • Accessibility: four-colour suits, high-contrast cards and reduced motion.

FreeCell FAQ

Is FreeCell Worlds free to play?

Yes. Classic FreeCell, the Daily Challenge and Voyage mode play free in your browser.

Do I need to download anything?

No. FreeCell Worlds runs in your web browser on desktop and mobile. You can also add it to your home screen like an app.

Can I play a specific FreeCell deal number?

Yes. Choose “Play a specific deal” on the title screen and enter any deal from 1 to 1,000,000, or leave it blank for a random deal.

What is Voyage mode?

Voyage is a run of seven FreeCell tables in a row. Clear each table to continue. There is no restart, and undos are shared across the whole run. A Daily Voyage is also available.

Are there hints and undo?

Yes. Press H for a hint and U to undo. A built-in solver powers the hints. You can also turn on auto-play to send safe cards to the foundations.

Is FreeCell Worlds accessible?

Settings include four-colour suits, high-contrast cards and a reduce-motion option.
