Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

This policy explains what Naat Ventures, Inc. ("we") collects when you use Free Cell Worlds at freecellworlds.com.

What we collect

  • Without an account: game settings and progress stay in your browser's local storage.
  • Play statistics: how the game is played, such as games started, won and abandoned, moves and time, hints and undos, the tables, themes, charms and challenges you encounter, and what you buy in Voyage markets. Each record carries your browser type, device type, approximate country (from your IP address, which we don't store with these records), the game version and the page that referred you. With your agreement where it's required (see below), we also keep a random device ID so visits can be connected, and link these records to your account if you sign in. We collect them ourselves and with Google Analytics. We don't use them for advertising and we don't run ad trackers.
  • With an account: your email address, name and profile picture (if you sign in with Google), a hashed password (if you use email sign-in), and your synced settings, stats and progress.
  • Custom themes: the prompts you write, the generated images and music, and our automated moderation results for each prompt.
  • Purchases: Stripe handles payment. We receive the purchase amount, pack, date and a Stripe reference. We never see or store your card number.
  • Support: messages you send us and any job details attached to them.
  • Technical: IP address and browser info in short-lived security logs and error reports, used for rate limiting, fraud prevention and fixing bugs.

How we use it

To run the Game, sync your progress, create and store your themes, process payments, prevent abuse and fraud, answer support requests, and send account emails (verification, password reset, "your theme is ready"). We use play statistics to balance and improve the Game: tuning difficulty, charms and challenges, finding bugs and slow devices, and understanding which features and purchases players value. We don't sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

Service providers

Cloudflare (hosting, database, file storage, email routing, play statistics), Stripe (payments and receipts), Google (sign-in, AI generation and Google Analytics), Resend (account emails) and Sentry (error reports). They process data only to provide their services to us. Google Analytics runs with advertising features, Google signals and ad personalization turned off.

Cookies and device storage

Essential cookies keep you signed in and protect against abuse. Your game, settings and privacy choice are saved in your browser's local storage.

For play statistics we store a random device ID in local storage, and Google Analytics sets its own cookies (_ga and _ga_*). If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we ask first and store neither unless you accept. If you decline, we only count anonymous game totals, with nothing stored on your device for statistics and no Google Analytics. We treat a browser's Global Privacy Control signal the same way, wherever you are.

Retention

We keep account data until you delete your account. Unchosen design options are deleted after a job finishes or expires. Security logs are kept for a short period. Purchase records are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires. Detailed play statistics are kept for up to 25 months; Google Analytics keeps its data for 14 months.

Your choices and rights

You can turn play statistics off completely in Settings → Privacy → Share play statistics; nothing is sent after that. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you can change your earlier choice there at any time. You can export your data or delete your account from the account menu, or email us. Deleting your account removes your profile, progress and themes, and the link between your account and your play statistics. California residents have the right to know, delete and correct personal information, and we don't sell or share it for cross-context advertising.

Children

Accounts are for ages 13 and up. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes and contact

We'll post updates here and notify account holders of material changes. Contact: support@freecellworlds.com, Naat Ventures, Inc., 2790 SW Hume St, Portland, OR 97219.
