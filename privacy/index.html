Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

This policy explains what Naat Ventures, Inc. ("we") collects when you use Free Cell Worlds at freecellworlds.com. Naat Ventures, Inc. is the data controller for this information.

What we collect

  • Without an account: game settings and progress stay in your browser's local storage.
  • Play statistics: how the game is played, such as games started, won and abandoned, moves and time, hints and undos, the tables, themes, charms and challenges you encounter, and what you buy in Voyage markets. Each record carries your browser type, device type, approximate country (from your IP address, which we don't store with these records), the game version and the page that referred you. With your agreement where it's required (see below), we also keep a random device ID so visits can be connected, and link these records to your account if you sign in. We collect them ourselves. Google Analytics is not loaded until a measurement id is configured. We don't use play statistics for advertising and we don't run ad trackers.
  • With an account: your email address, name and profile picture (if you sign in with Google), a hashed password (if you use email sign-in), and your synced settings, stats, current deal, Voyage run and tutorial progress. Your account is identified by a random ID, not your email address.
  • Account security: a short-lived count of failed sign-in attempts for your email, and a Cloudflare Turnstile check when you sign up, sign in with a password, or reset a password.
  • Custom themes: the prompts you write, the generated images and music, and our automated moderation results for each prompt.
  • Purchases: Stripe handles payment. We receive the purchase amount, pack, date and a Stripe reference. We never see or store your card number.
  • Support: messages you send us and any job details attached to them.
  • Technical: IP address and browser info in short-lived security logs and error reports, used for rate limiting, fraud prevention and fixing bugs.

How we use it

To run the Game, sync your progress between your devices, create and store your themes, process payments, prevent abuse and fraud, answer support requests, and send account emails (verification, password reset, "your theme is ready"). We use play statistics to balance and improve the Game: tuning difficulty, charms and challenges, finding bugs and slow devices, and understanding which features and purchases players value. We don't sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

Service providers

Cloudflare (hosting, database, file storage, email routing, Turnstile human verification, and play statistics), Stripe (payments and receipts), Google (sign-in and AI generation), Resend (account emails) and Sentry (error reports). They process data only to provide their services to us. Google Analytics, when a measurement id is configured, runs with advertising features, Google signals and ad personalization turned off.

When you choose a password, we check it against Have I Been Pwned’s list of passwords exposed in data breaches. Only the first five characters of a hash of the password leave our servers, never the password itself.

Cookies and device storage

Essential cookies keep you signed in and protect against abuse. Your game, settings and privacy choice are saved in your browser's local storage.

For play statistics we store a random device ID in local storage. Google Analytics sets its own cookies (_ga and _ga_*) only when it is loaded and you have accepted. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we ask first and store neither unless you accept. If you decline, we only count anonymous game totals, with nothing stored on your device for statistics and no Google Analytics. We treat a browser's Global Privacy Control signal the same way, wherever you are.

Retention

We keep account data until you delete your account. Email sign-ups that are never confirmed are deleted after 24 hours. Sign-in sessions end after 60 days without use. Unchosen design options are deleted after a job finishes or expires. Security logs are kept for a short period. Purchase records are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires. Detailed play statistics are kept for up to 25 months. When Google Analytics is configured, it keeps its data for 14 months.

Your choices and rights

You can turn play statistics off completely in Settings → Privacy → Share play statistics; nothing is sent after that. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you can change your earlier choice there at any time. You can download your data or delete your account from the account menu (Sign in, then Account), or email us. Deleting your account removes your profile, synced progress, themes and unused tokens, and the link between your account and your play statistics; we confirm the request by email first. Progress saved in your browser stays on your device until you clear it. California residents have the right to know, delete and correct personal information, and we don't sell or share it for cross-context advertising.

Your rights in the EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR)

We use your information on these legal bases:

  • Contract: to provide your account, sync your progress, create your themes and process your purchases.
  • Consent: for play statistics that use a device ID or Google Analytics, where the law asks us to ask first.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep accounts secure (rate limits, human verification, breached-password checks), prevent fraud and abuse, fix bugs, and understand anonymous game totals where consent is not required.
  • Legal obligation: to keep purchase records for tax and accounting law.

You have the right to access, correct, delete or download your personal data, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office). For any of these, email gdpr@freecellworlds.com. We may ask you to confirm the request from your account email, and we respond within one month.

We are based in the United States and so are our service providers' main systems, so your data is processed in the US. Where the law requires, these transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the provider's certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Children

Accounts are for ages 13 and up. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes and contact

We'll post updates here and notify account holders of material changes. Contact: support@freecellworlds.com (privacy and data requests: gdpr@freecellworlds.com), Naat Ventures, Inc., 2790 SW Hume St, Portland, OR 97219.
