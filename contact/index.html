Custom Themes

Free Cell Worlds is free to play, no account needed. Signed-in players can create their own private board themes from a text prompt: a hero card, table, card backs and an original music track.

Theme Tokens (one token makes one theme, with up to 3 design options to choose from):

  • 1 token, $4
  • 3 tokens, $10
  • 6 tokens, $15

Prices in USD. Tokens don't expire. See our Terms of Service and Refund Policy.
